📌 壹、核心詞彙 Core Vocabulary

學測英文詞彙範圍以大考中心公布的「高中英文參考詞彙表」Level 1～5（約 7,000 字）為主。

The GSAT English vocabulary is based on the CEEC Reference Word List, Levels 1-5 (approximately 7,000 words).

一、詞彙學習策略 Vocabulary Learning Strategies

⭐ 高效背單字方法
  • 字根字首法 (Word Roots & Prefixes)：利用字根拆解單字，如 "un-" = 不、"re-" = 再、"-tion" = 名詞化
  • 語境記憶法 (Contextual Learning)：在句子中記憶單字，而非孤立背誦
  • 聯想法 (Association)：將新字與已知字詞建立聯繫
  • 間隔重複 (Spaced Repetition)：定期複習，遺忘曲線前再強化

二、常見字首 Common Prefixes

字首 Prefix意義 Meaning範例 Examples

pre-之前 (before)preview（預覽）, predict（預測）, prevent（預防）
dis-不、分離 (not, apart)disagree（不同意）, discover（發現）, disappear（消失）
mis-錯誤 (wrong)mistake（錯誤）, misunderstand（誤解）, mislead（誤導）
over-過度 (excessively)overwork（過勞）, overcome（克服）, overlook（忽略）
inter-之間 (between)international（國際的）, interact（互動）, internet（網路）
trans-跨越 (across)transport（運輸）, transform（轉變）, translate（翻譯）

super-超越 (above, beyond)superhero（超級英雄）, supermarket（超市）

📌 貳、十二時態 Twelve Tenses

英文時態由時間（過去/現在/未來）和態（簡單/進行/完成/完成進行）組成，共 12 種。

English tenses are formed by combining TIME (past/present/future) with ASPECT (simple/progressive/perfect/perfect progressive).

時態 Tense結構 Structure用法 Usage範例 Example
簡單現在式
Simple Present		V / V-s習慣、事實、真理
Habits, facts, universal truths		She speaks English fluently.
現在進行式
Present Progressive		am/is/are + V-ing正在進行的動作
Actions happening now		She is studying for the exam.
現在完成式
Present Perfect		have/has + p.p.過去到現在的經驗或結果
Experience or result up to now		I have visited Japan twice.
現在完成進行式
Present Perfect Progressive		have/has been + V-ing從過去持續到現在且仍在進行
Action started in past, continuing now		He has been waiting for two hours.
簡單過去式
Simple Past		V-ed / V₂過去的事實、動作
Completed past actions		They arrived yesterday.
過去進行式
Past Progressive		was/were + V-ing過去某時正在進行
Action in progress at a past time		I was reading when she called.
過去完成式
Past Perfect		had + p.p.過去的過去
Action before another past action		He had left before I arrived.
過去完成進行式
Past Perfect Progressive		had been + V-ing在過去某時點之前一直持續的動作She had been working all day.
簡單未來式
Simple Future		will + V未來的計畫或預測
Future plans or predictions		It will rain tomorrow.
未來進行式
Future Progressive		will be + V-ing未來某時正在進行
Action in progress at a future time		I will be sleeping at midnight.
未來完成式
Future Perfect		will have + p.p.在未來某時點前完成
Completed before a future time		By June, I will have graduated.
未來完成進行式
Future Perfect Progressive		will have been + V-ing到未來某時一直持續的動作By 2027, I will have been studying for 3 years.
💡 學測重點 Key for GSAT：最常考的時態為簡單現在、簡單過去、現在完成、過去完成。注意 since + 過去時間 和 for + 一段時間 搭配完成式。

📌 參、重要句型結構 Sentence Patterns

一、五大句型 Five Basic Sentence Patterns

句型結構 Structure範例 Example
S + V主詞 + 不及物動詞The baby cried.（嬰兒哭了）
S + V + C主詞 + 連綴動詞 + 補語She looks happy.（她看起來很開心）
S + V + O主詞 + 及物動詞 + 受詞He likes music.（他喜歡音樂）
S + V + O + O主詞 + 動詞 + 間接受詞 + 直接受詞She gave me a book.（她給了我一本書）
S + V + O + C主詞 + 動詞 + 受詞 + 受詞補語They elected him president.（他們選他為總統）

二、被動語態 Passive Voice

被動語態的結構：be + 過去分詞 (p.p.)，強調「承受動作者」而非「執行者」。

Passive voice structure: be + past participle, emphasizing the receiver of the action.

時態主動 Active被動 Passive
Simple PresentHe writes the letter.The letter is written by him.
Simple PastHe wrote the letter.The letter was written by him.
Present PerfectHe has written the letter.The letter has been written by him.
ModalsHe can write the letter.The letter can be written by him.

三、比較句型 Comparisons

句型結構範例
原級比較as + adj/adv + asHe is as tall as his brother.（他和他哥一樣高）
比較級adj-er / more adj + thanShe is smarter than her classmates.（她比同學聰明）
最高級the adj-est / the most adjHe is the tallest in the class.（他是班上最高的）
倍數X times as ... as / X times adj-er thanThis building is three times as tall as that one.
The + 比較, the + 比較The more..., the more...The harder you study, the better grades you get.

📌 肆、子句 Clauses

一、名詞子句 Noun Clauses

名詞子句在句中當作名詞使用（主詞、受詞、補語）。

A noun clause functions as a noun in the sentence (subject, object, or complement).

引導詞範例說明
thatThat he passed the exam surprised everyone.that 引導的名詞子句當主詞（他通過考試令大家驚訝）
whether/ifI don't know whether he will come.表示「是否」（我不知道他是否會來）
wh- 疑問詞Tell me what you need.間接問句（告訴我你需要什麼）

二、形容詞子句（關係子句）Adjective Clauses (Relative Clauses)

用關係代名詞連接，修飾前面的名詞（先行詞）。

關係代名詞先行詞範例
who人（主格）The girl who sits next to me is my friend.（坐我旁邊的女孩是我朋友）
whom人（受格）The teacher whom I respect most retired.（我最尊敬的老師退休了）
whose人/物（所有格）The man whose car was stolen called the police.（車被偷的人報警了）
which物The book which I bought is interesting.（我買的書很有趣）
that人/物（通用）This is the best movie that I have ever seen.（這是我看過最好的電影）
💡 限定 vs 非限定 (Restrictive vs Non-restrictive)：非限定用逗號隔開，提供補充資訊；限定子句不用逗號，用以識別先行詞。Non-restrictive clauses use commas and provide extra info.

三、副詞子句 Adverb Clauses

類型常用連接詞範例
時間 Timewhen, while, before, after, since, until, as soon asAfter I finish my homework, I will go out.（做完功課後我會出門）
原因 Reasonbecause, since, as, now thatBecause it rained, we stayed home.（因為下雨，我們待在家）
條件 Conditionif, unless, as long as, provided thatIf you study hard, you will pass.（如果你努力，就會通過）
讓步 Concessionalthough, though, even though, whileAlthough he is young, he is very mature.（雖然他年輕，但很成熟）
目的 Purposeso that, in order thatHe studied hard so that he could pass.（他努力讀書以便通過）
結果 Resultso...that, such...thatShe was so tired that she fell asleep.（她太累了以至於睡著了）

📌 伍、特殊文法 Special Grammar

一、假設語氣 Subjunctive Mood

假設語氣用於與事實相反或不太可能發生的情況。動詞時態需「退後一步」。

The subjunctive mood expresses unreal conditions. Verb tenses shift back one step.

類型If 子句主要子句範例
與現在事實相反
Contrary to present		If + S + V-ed / wereS + would/could/might + VIf I were you, I would study harder.（如果我是你，我會更努力）
與過去事實相反
Contrary to past		If + S + had + p.p.S + would/could/might + have + p.p.If he had studied, he would have passed.（如果他有讀書，他就會通過了）
未來不太可能
Unlikely future		If + S + should + V / were to + VS + would/could + VIf it should rain, we would cancel the trip.（萬一下雨，我們就取消旅行）

二、分詞構句 Participial Constructions

分詞構句是將副詞子句簡化，省略「連接詞 + 主詞」。

Participial constructions simplify adverb clauses by removing the conjunction and subject.

原句簡化後說明
Because she felt tired, she went to bed early.Feeling tired, she went to bed early.主動用 V-ing（主詞相同，省略連接詞和主詞）
Because he was bitten by a dog, he went to the hospital.Bitten by a dog, he went to the hospital.被動用 p.p.（省略 being）

三、倒裝句 Inversion

類型結構範例
否定副詞在句首Never / Seldom / Hardly + 助V + S + VNever have I seen such a beautiful sunset.（我從未見過如此美麗的日落）
Only + 副詞在句首Only + 副詞片語 + 助V + S + VOnly then did I realize the truth.（直到那時我才意識到真相）
So / NeitherSo + 助V + S / Neither + 助V + SI like coffee. — So do I.（我也是）

四、不定詞與動名詞 Infinitives vs Gerunds

分類常見動詞說明
只接 to Vwant, hope, decide, plan, promise, expect, agree, refuseShe decided to go.（她決定去）
只接 V-ingenjoy, finish, mind, avoid, practice, suggest, consider, denyI enjoy reading.（我喜歡閱讀）
兩者皆可（意思相同）like, love, hate, begin, start, continueShe likes to swim / likes swimming.
兩者皆可（意思不同）remember, forget, stop, try, regretremember to V（記得去做）vs remember V-ing（記得做過）

📌 柒、中翻英技巧 Translation Tips

學測翻譯題通常為兩個中文句子翻譯成英文，考文法與詞彙的綜合運用。

The GSAT translation section requires translating two Chinese sentences into English.

翻譯步驟 Translation Steps

⭐ 翻譯四步驟
  • Step 1 確認時態：先判斷句子的時態（過去/現在/未來/完成）
  • Step 2 找出主詞與動詞：中文常省略主詞，翻譯時必須補上
  • Step 3 建立句型：套用五大句型，注意主詞動詞一致性
  • Step 4 檢查細節：單複數、冠詞(a/an/the)、介系詞、拼字

常見翻譯考點 Common Translation Topics

考點中文英文
it 虛主詞對我們來說很重要It is important for us to...
關係子句住在臺北的人們People who live in Taipei...
被動語態這座橋是在1900年建造的This bridge was built in 1900.
比較級運動越多，越健康The more you exercise, the healthier you become.
現在完成科技已經改變了我們的生活Technology has changed our lives.

📌 捌、英文作文指南 Writing Guide

學測英文作文通常為看圖寫作或主題式寫作，約 120 字。

一、作文結構 Essay Structure

段落功能 Function寫法 How to Write
Introduction 引言吸引讀者、提出主題用 hook（問句/引述/場景描述）開頭，末句為 thesis statement（主題句）
Body 主體發展論點、舉例說明每段一個主題句 + 支持細節 + 例子。用轉折語連接
Conclusion 結論總結重點、呼應主題重述主旨（換句話說），不引入新論點

二、加分句型 Advanced Sentence Patterns

  • 強調句：It is... that...（正是...才...）
  • 分詞構句：V-ing..., S + V...（...著，...）
  • 倒裝句：Not only did... but also...（不僅...而且...）
  • 假設語氣：If I were..., I would...（如果我是...，我會...）
  • 名詞子句：What matters most is...（最重要的是...）
⚠️ 常見扣分點 Common Deductions：拼字錯誤(spelling errors)、主動詞不一致(S-V agreement)、時態不一致(tense inconsistency)、中式英文(Chinglish)、缺少冠詞(missing articles)。

玖、進階探討與滿分秘笈 🏆

1. 長難句結構拆解 (Parsing Complex Sentences)

遇到超過三行的長難句時，學會找出句子的「主幹（S+V）」是解題關鍵。

  • 分詞構句陷阱：Seeing the dog, he ran away. 學測常考被動或完成式的分詞構句，例如 Having been scolded, the boy cried. (因為被罵了，男孩哭了)。解題時請還原主詞，判斷主動或被動。
  • 倒裝句型：Not until he came back did I go to bed. (直到他回來我才去睡覺)。注意助動詞的提前，這在閱讀測驗中常被用來強調語氣。

2. 英文寫作實戰：高分轉折詞與亮點句型

作文要拿高分，避免通篇使用 and, but, so。建議替換為：

  • 表示遞進：Furthermore, Moreover, In addition
  • 表示轉折：Nevertheless, However, Conversely
  • 亮點句型：使用強調句 It is... that... 或假設語氣 If I had studied harder, I would have passed. 來增加句型多樣性。
