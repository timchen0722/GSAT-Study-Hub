📌 壹、核心詞彙 Core Vocabulary🔊

學測英文詞彙範圍以大考中心公布的「高中英文參考詞彙表」Level 1～5（約 7,000 字）為主。

The GSAT English vocabulary is based on the CEEC Reference Word List, Levels 1-5 (approximately 7,000 words).🔊

一、詞彙學習策略 Vocabulary Learning Strategies🔊

⭐ 高效背單字方法
  • 字根字首法 (Word Roots & Prefixes🔊)：利用字根拆解單字，如 "un-" = 不、"re-" = 再、"-tion" = 名詞化
  • 語境記憶法 (Contextual Learning🔊)：在句子中記憶單字，而非孤立背誦
  • 聯想法 (Association🔊)：將新字與已知字詞建立聯繫
  • 間隔重複 (Spaced Repetition🔊)：定期複習，遺忘曲線前再強化

📌 貳、十二時態 Twelve Tenses🔊

英文時態由時間（過去/現在/未來）和態（簡單/進行/完成/完成進行）組成，共 12 種。

English tenses are formed by combining TIME (past/present/future) with ASPECT (simple/progressive/perfect/perfect progressive).🔊

時態 Tense🔊結構 Structure🔊用法 Usage🔊範例 Example🔊
簡單現在式
Simple Present🔊		V / V-s🔊習慣、事實、真理
Habits, facts, universal truths🔊		She speaks English fluently.🔊
現在進行式
Present Progressive🔊		am/is/are + V-ing🔊正在進行的動作
Actions happening now🔊		She is studying for the exam.🔊
現在完成式
Present Perfect🔊		have/has + p.p.🔊過去到現在的經驗或結果
Experience or result up to now🔊		I have visited Japan twice.🔊
現在完成進行式
Present Perfect Progressive🔊		have/has been + V-ing🔊從過去持續到現在且仍在進行
Action started in past, continuing now🔊		He has been waiting for two hours.🔊
簡單過去式
Simple Past🔊		V-ed / V₂🔊過去的事實、動作
Completed past actions🔊		They arrived yesterday.🔊
過去進行式
Past Progressive🔊		was/were + V-ing🔊過去某時正在進行
Action in progress at a past time🔊		I was reading when she called.🔊
過去完成式
Past Perfect🔊		had + p.p.🔊過去的過去
Action before another past action🔊		He had left before I arrived.🔊
過去完成進行式
Past Perfect Progressive🔊		had been + V-ing🔊在過去某時點之前一直持續的動作
Action continuing up to a past time🔊		She had been working all day.🔊
簡單未來式
Simple Future🔊		will + V🔊未來的計畫或預測
Future plans or predictions🔊		It will rain tomorrow.🔊
未來進行式
Future Progressive🔊		will be + V-ing🔊未來某時正在進行
Action in progress at a future time🔊		I will be sleeping at midnight.🔊
未來完成式
Future Perfect🔊		will have + p.p.🔊在未來某時點前完成
Completed before a future time🔊		By June, I will have graduated.🔊
未來完成進行式
Future Perfect Progressive🔊		will have been + V-ing🔊到未來某時一直持續的動作
Action continuing up to a future time🔊		By 2027, I will have been studying for 3 years.🔊
💡 學測重點 Key for GSAT：最常考的時態為簡單現在、簡單過去、現在完成、過去完成。注意 since + 過去時間 和 for + 一段時間 搭配完成式。

📌 參、重要句型結構 Sentence Patterns🔊

一、五大句型 Five Basic Sentence Patterns🔊

句型結構 Structure🔊範例 Example🔊
S + V🔊主詞 + 不及物動詞The baby cried.🔊（嬰兒哭了）
S + V + C🔊主詞 + 連綴動詞 + 補語She looks happy.🔊（她看起來很開心）
S + V + O🔊主詞 + 及物動詞 + 受詞He likes music.🔊（他喜歡音樂）
S + V + O + O🔊主詞 + 動詞 + 間接受詞 + 直接受詞She gave me a book.🔊（她給了我一本書）
S + V + O + C🔊主詞 + 動詞 + 受詞 + 受詞補語They elected him president.🔊（他們選他為總統）

二、被動語態 Passive Voice🔊

被動語態的結構：be + 過去分詞 (p.p.)，強調「承受動作者」而非「執行者」。

Passive voice structure: be + past participle, emphasizing the receiver of the action.🔊

時態主動 Active🔊被動 Passive🔊
Simple Present🔊He writes the letter.🔊The letter is written by him.🔊
Simple Past🔊He wrote the letter.🔊The letter was written by him.🔊
Present Perfect🔊He has written the letter.🔊The letter has been written by him.🔊
Modals🔊He can write the letter.🔊The letter can be written by him.🔊

三、比較句型 Comparisons🔊

句型結構範例
原級比較as + adj/adv + as🔊He is as tall as his brother.🔊
比較級adj-er / more adj + than🔊She is smarter than her classmates.🔊
最高級the adj-est / the most adj🔊He is the tallest in the class.🔊
倍數X times as ... as / X times adj-er than🔊This building is three times as tall as that one.🔊
The + 比較, the + 比較🔊The more..., the more...🔊The harder you study, the better grades you get.🔊

📌 肆、子句 Clauses🔊

一、名詞子句 Noun Clauses🔊

名詞子句在句中當作名詞使用（主詞、受詞、補語）。

A noun clause functions as a noun in the sentence (subject, object, or complement).🔊

引導詞範例說明
that🔊That he passed the exam surprised everyone.🔊that noun clause as subject（他通過考試令大家驚訝）
whether/if🔊I don't know whether he will come.🔊表示「是否」（我不知道他是否會來）
wh- 疑問詞🔊Tell me what you need.🔊間接問句（告訴我你需要什麼）

二、形容詞子句（關係子句）Adjective Clauses (Relative Clauses)🔊

用關係代名詞連接，修飾前面的名詞（先行詞）。

關係代名詞先行詞範例
who🔊人（主格）The girl who sits next to me is my friend.🔊
whom🔊人（受格）The teacher whom I respect most retired.🔊
whose🔊人/物（所有格）The man whose car was stolen called the police.🔊
which🔊物The book which I bought is interesting.🔊
that🔊人/物（通用）This is the best movie that I have ever seen.🔊
💡 限定 vs 非限定 (Restrictive vs Non-restrictive🔊)：非限定用逗號隔開，提供補充資訊；限定子句不用逗號，用以識別先行詞。Non-restrictive clauses use commas and provide extra info.🔊

三、副詞子句 Adverb Clauses🔊

類型常用連接詞範例
時間 Timewhen, while, before, after, since, until, as soon as🔊After I finish my homework, I will go out.🔊
原因 Reasonbecause, since, as, now that🔊Because it rained, we stayed home.🔊
條件 Conditionif, unless, as long as, provided thatIf you study hard, you will pass.🔊
讓步 Concessionalthough, though, even though, whileAlthough he is young, he is very mature.🔊
目的 Purposeso that, in order thatHe studied hard so that he could pass.🔊
結果 Resultso...that, such...thatShe was so tired that she fell asleep.🔊

📌 伍、特殊文法 Special Grammar🔊

一、假設語氣 Subjunctive Mood🔊

假設語氣用於與事實相反或不太可能發生的情況。動詞時態需「退後一步」。

The subjunctive mood expresses unreal conditions. Verb tenses shift back one step.🔊

類型If 子句主要子句範例
與現在事實相反
Contrary to present		If + S + V-ed / wereS + would/could/might + VIf I were you, I would study harder.🔊
與過去事實相反
Contrary to past		If + S + had + p.p.S + would/could/might + have + p.p.If he had studied, he would have🔊 passed.🔊
未來不太可能
Unlikely future		If + S + should + V / were to + VS + would/could + VIf it should rain, we would cancel the trip.🔊

二、分詞構句 Participial Constructions🔊

分詞構句是將副詞子句簡化，省略「連接詞 + 主詞」。

Participial constructions simplify adverb clauses by removing the conjunction and subject.🔊

原句簡化後說明
Because she felt tired, she went to bed early.🔊Feeling tired, she went to bed early.🔊主動用 V-ing🔊（主詞相同，省略連接詞和主詞）
Because he was bitten by a dog, he went to the hospital.🔊Bitten by a dog, he went to the hospital.🔊被動用 p.p.（省略 being）

三、倒裝句 Inversion🔊

類型結構範例
否定副詞在句首Never / Seldom / Hardly + 助V + S + VNever have I seen such a beautiful sunset.🔊
Only + 副詞在句首Only + 副詞片語 + 助V + S + VOnly then did🔊 I realize🔊 the truth.🔊
So / NeitherSo + 助V + S / Neither + 助V + SI like coffee. — So do I.🔊

四、不定詞與動名詞 Infinitives vs Gerunds🔊

分類常見動詞說明
只接 to Vwant, hope, decide, plan, promise, expect, agree, refuseShe decided to go.🔊
只接 V-ingenjoy, finish, mind, avoid, practice, suggest, consider, denyI enjoy reading.🔊
兩者皆可（意思相同）like, love, hate, begin, start, continue🔊She likes to swim / likes swimming.
兩者皆可（意思不同）remember, forget, stop, try, regretremember to V🔊（記得去做）vs remember V-ing🔊（記得做過）

題型說明答題策略 Strategy🔊

📌 柒、中翻英技巧 Translation Tips🔊

學測翻譯題通常為兩個中文句子翻譯成英文，考文法與詞彙的綜合運用。

The GSAT translation section requires translating two Chinese sentences into English.🔊

翻譯步驟 Translation Steps🔊

⭐ 翻譯四步驟
  • Step 1🔊 確認時態：先判斷句子的時態（過去/現在/未來/完成）
  • Step 2🔊 找出主詞與動詞：中文常省略主詞，翻譯時必須補上
  • Step 3🔊 建立句型：套用五大句型，注意主詞動詞一致性
  • Step 4🔊 檢查細節：單複數、冠詞(a/an/the)、介系詞、拼字

常見翻譯考點 Common Translation Topics🔊

考點中文英文
it 虛主詞對我們來說很重要It is important for us to...🔊
關係子句住在臺北的人們People who live in Taipei...🔊
被動語態這座橋是在1900年建造的This bridge was built in 1900.🔊
比較級運動越多，越健康The more you exercise, the healthier you become.🔊
現在完成科技已經改變了我們的生活Technology has changed our lives.🔊

📌 捌、英文作文指南 Writing Guide🔊

學測英文作文通常為看圖寫作或主題式寫作，約 120 字。

一、作文結構 Essay Structure🔊

段落功能 Function🔊寫法 How to Write🔊
Introduction 引言吸引讀者、提出主題用 hook🔊（問句/引述/場景描述）開頭，末句為 thesis statement🔊（主題句）
Body Body🔊發展論點、舉例說明每段一個主題句 + 支持細節 + 例子。用轉折語連接
Conclusion Conclusion🔊總結重點、呼應主題重述主旨（換句話說），不引入新論點

二、加分句型 Advanced Sentence Patterns🔊

  • 強調句：It is... that...🔊（正是...才...）
  • 分詞構句：V-ing..., S + V...🔊（...著，...）
  • 倒裝句：Not only did🔊... but also...🔊（不僅...而且...）
  • 假設語氣：If I were..., I would...🔊（如果我是...，我會...）
  • 名詞子句：What matters most is...🔊（最重要的是...）
⚠️ 常見扣分點 Common Deductions🔊：拼字錯誤 (spelling errors🔊)、主動詞不一致 (S-V agreement🔊)、時態不一致 (tense inconsistency🔊)、中式英文 (Chinglish🔊)、缺少冠詞 (missing articles🔊)。

玖、進階探討與滿分秘笈 🏆

1. 長難句結構拆解 (Parsing Complex Sentences🔊🔊)

遇到超過三行的長難句時，學會找出句子的「主幹（S+V）」是解題關鍵。

  • 分詞構句陷阱：Seeing the dog, he ran away.🔊 學測常考被動或完成式的分詞構句，例如 Having been scolded, the boy cried.🔊 (因為被罵了，男孩哭了)。解題時請還原主詞，判斷主動或被動。
  • 倒裝句型：Not until he came back did🔊 I go to bed.🔊 (直到他回來我才去睡覺)。注意助動詞的提前，這在閱讀測驗中常被用來強調語氣。

2. 英文寫作實戰：高分轉折詞與亮點句型

作文要拿高分，避免通篇使用 and, but, so🔊。建議替換為：

  • 表示遞進：Furthermore, Moreover, In addition🔊
  • 表示轉折：Nevertheless, However, Conversely🔊
  • 亮點句型：使用強調句 It is... that...🔊 或假設語氣 If I had studied harder, I would have🔊 passed.🔊 來增加句型多樣性。

拾、學測專武：歷屆大考趨勢與深度剖析 🎯

1. 學測文法必殺技：三大魔王公式化

學測克漏字與文法題中最常出現的三大句型，只要套用公式即可秒殺：

🔥 必殺技 1：與過去事實相反的假設語氣
Step
If + S + had + V.p.p, S + would have🔊 + V.p.p🔊
這是最常考的句型。若看到 would have🔊，前面必找 had V.p.p。
進階
Had🔊 + S + V.p.p, S + would have🔊 + V.p.p🔊
學測超愛考「省略 If」的倒裝！只要看到 Had🔊 在句首，99% 是這個句型。
🔥 必殺技 2：否定副詞放句首（倒裝句）
公式
Never / Seldom / Not until... + 助動詞/be動詞 + S + V🔊
一看到否定詞在句首，立刻將選項中「沒有倒裝」的刪除。
範例
Not until he came back did🔊 I realize🔊 the truth.🔊
注意助動詞 did🔊 後面必須接原形動詞 realize🔊。

2. 英文作文滿分模板 (English Essay Templates🔊)

學測英文作文最重要的是「邏輯連貫」與「亮點句型」。

  • 看圖說故事 (Picture Story🔊)：
    • 開頭必備時間與人物設定：It was a typical sunny Sunday afternoon when [主角] decided to...🔊
    • 轉折語氣（引發衝突）：Out of nowhere, / To his astonishment, / Unexpectedly, ...🔊
    • 結尾感想：This incident taught him a valuable lesson: ...🔊
  • 論說文 (Opinion Essay🔊)：
    • 開頭引入背景（Topic Sentence🔊）：In recent years, the issue of [主題] has sparked widespread debate. Personally, I firmly believe that...🔊
    • 舉例說明：Take [例子] for instance, ... / A prime example of this is...🔊
    • 結尾重申：In conclusion, weighing the pros and cons, it is evident that...🔊
